Dig Dug

The Atari 7800 Dig Dug, ported to the Picocomputer 6502.

Move:   Arrows or WASD, or the d-pad or left stick
Pump:   Space, Z or Ctrl, or A, B, X or Y
Start:  Enter or Start
Pause:  P
Mute:   M
Quit:   Escape

On the title screen, Up and Down choose 1 or 2 players, and Left and
Right choose the level: Teddy is easy and Pineapple is normal.
